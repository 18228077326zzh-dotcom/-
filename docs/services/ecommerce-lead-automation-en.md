## FAQ

**Do you need access to my store account?**

No. The listed service works with XLSX or CSV files you are authorized to export. Do not send passwords, cookies, tokens, or payment data.

**Will it send messages automatically?**

No. It creates drafts. Only a response reviewed and confirmed by a person enters the sendable export.

**Do you guarantee classification accuracy or sales results?**

No. Delivery is validated against the examples and rules agreed before the order, and results still require human review.

**Will I receive source code?**

The public base case is already available under the MIT License. Paid work covers field mapping, rule calibration, testing, and delivery. Licensing for customer-specific additions is agreed before the order.

**How should I protect customer data?**

The public free-preview issue accepts only fully synthetic data. For paid work, provide only fields necessary for the agreed task and confirm any anonymized-file handling through the order platform's private channel.
