# Market-fit notes

Observation date: 2026-09-27. Public listings and service cards change over time; figures below are directional observations, not promised budgets or成交 prices.

## Why these two projects

- Public service searches on [猪八戒 AI services](https://www.zbj.com/fw/?kw=%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD), [data analysis](https://www.zbj.com/fw/?kw=%E6%95%B0%E6%8D%AE%E5%88%86%E6%9E%90), and [mini-program development](https://www.zbj.com/fw/?kw=%E5%B0%8F%E7%A8%8B%E5%BA%8F%E5%BC%80%E5%8F%91) showed recurring demand around AI efficiency tools, order management, commerce, appointments, and operational reporting. Displayed service-card prices are often starting prices or lead-generation prices, not complete custom-project budgets.
- [程序员客栈整包开发](https://www.proginn.com/b/outsource) publicly describes a delivery flow covering requirement clarification, implementation, testing, acceptance, and maintenance. The READMEs therefore make scope, acceptance, and maintenance boundaries explicit.
- Public [Freelancer OpenAI](https://www.freelancer.com/jobs/openai/), [API](https://www.freelancer.com/jobs/api/), [Shopify](https://www.freelancer.com/jobs/shopify/), and [WordPress](https://www.freelancer.com/jobs/wordpress/) listings showed demand for AI/data automation, reporting portals, e-commerce integrations, and business MVPs. Bid averages and bid counts are competition signals only.
- Upwork and Fiverr live listings were blocked by anti-bot/login controls during the review, so no unverified real-time prices from those platforms are presented here.

## Positioning

1. **E-commerce Lead Automation** demonstrates spreadsheet ingestion, domain rules, optional LLM integration, human review, export safety, and a concrete client workflow.
2. **Market Research Brief** demonstrates safe public-source ingestion, evidence traceability, model fallback behavior, SSRF defenses, and portable report export.

These projects are intentionally narrower than a generic “AI platform.” A buyer can understand the input, output, customization points, and acceptance criteria in a short demo.
